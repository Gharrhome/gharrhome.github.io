The Suno Backstab (Nov 2026)

I would say that the future of Suno being a music sharing platform like Youtube or Nico-Nico is not looking good; but it may still be a music producers favorite AI Music Studio. It is just that music producers may have to find other ways to share their music; as Suno has become glitchy, and undependable with their no opt-out pop up windows that block the music producers page.

🗡 #Global #Music #News: While Suno might not be saying anything official about it to its users (the music producers); the Suno Music Platform has a popup that appears in certain browsers (a problem that does not occur with Nico-Nico or Youtube); and that popup can block access to the producers page and even lower sound levels if you don't agree to what it asks (no opt-out options).

Not all browsers seem effected; Suno seems to have upgraded to a higher level of security, and monitoring people using cookies to a similar or higher level that X uses; which also only works in some browsers also.

As I said Youtube and Nico-Nico does not do this; and the move may indicate a re-branding of Suno where the music is placed behind log-in and pay walls; but it remains to be seen what will actually happen; for the moment I have been able to sort of make it work in one of the browser I use; so Suno has reduced it reach; but the access is still there; but the press to play has hurdles that many fans will have to jump to listen to the Suno music; and it is quite possible that many of those fans will simply turn to other platforms when faced with agressive popups.

My own stance is to keep re-posting Suno Music pages and promoting music in Suno; but Suno will no longer have my 100%; I will start to prioritize YouTube, Nico-Nico, and a new platform called Aethrmusic (but many Suno Music Producers seem to avoid it, so it may have some issues that I am not aware of yet).
