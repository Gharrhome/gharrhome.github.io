Things of interest; My first post

My first comment (first post). 🦊📋🧝‍♀️ "I have several websites, and I want this one to do something different from the others. I have not yet decided what this difference is... ❧

I discussed this with Chatgpt, but as usual I had to be the "team-guide" to make sure the conversation went in a meaningful direction. I didn't store the conversation, but came to the conclusion that I cover a lot of ground, and the differences in this website will be more about a change of flavor, then the complete change to a concept, such a focused tech website, as suggested by Chatgpt; because by focusing on a very narrow topic, I reduce what I do, and I don't think that is what I am about, and it will not work out well for me in the long run.

I will continue to do what I always do, it is the flavor that will make all the difference.

I think that for a start, I will just post stuff, and write articles, and do whatever else I can imagine doing with this type of website. My initial aim is to "Dream Boldly."

A lot of music producers have complained that Suno and possibly other music platforms don't give enough attention to new music producers. On my social networks I do try and share music from new producers... but my connections, or the social network has to put these new music producers onto my time line or I will not notice them. Currently I am not a member of Suno, so I can't do searches for the music there. I don't like joining platforms to find out about music, I prefer music that is easily shared, and comes in from the wild rivers. Some music producers are making podcasts, and playlists from several different music producers, and I might be able to follow these people, or even get into this gig myself. I have also made a question and answer tool that makes use of agents, and I could also get into news and opinions from the global community about various music. 2026-2027 is opening many different and new possibilities, so who knows what is possible in the near future, and what I will end up doing on this website. I often have the ability to create things using AI that makes me able to do things that I thought were impossible before, and that is why "Dreaming Boldly" is so important, as I now have the ability to become a very powerful solo operator, and ideas person; and we all do this, because that is what defines the abundance era that we are heading into.

However here is two possible directions that can give flavor to this website, and my other website. But my initial "starting steps" experiments might reveal other paths I have not thought about yet.

I think one of my websites will cover all the old Suno music that has been made, as that music does not really get the attention it deserves, and older versions of Suno still sound very good. I know there are fans sill listening to music that has been out there for decades or longer. We are fans, what can you expect! The problem with this idea is that AI music producers often leave and just simply disappear; but still this idea has a lot of potential. A lot of music producers have asked that they want there music to be played more then once, and we are a nostalgic lot, so i think the classics will always get some love from the old fans.

The other website... well I thought I might focus on a few artists and producers, unfortunately Suno no longer lists all their music, so that is going to be a tough job, but the music seems to still be out there for us to share, even after all this time, and the fact that some artists and produces have amassed a huge body of work makes me wonder how they can store it all. It will be like a Japanese Idol fan website, but there will be several stars that will interest me, not just one. As mentioned above AI music producers come and go (just like real idols and stars); so the AI music producers I cover will also always be changing.

Both websites will probably have things in common also, as other discussions are always interesting to us all, no matter what website we visit. I have buttons for dances, techno and vocaloid here; so that will be covered in this website. The other website, well that is still open to all sorts of things.

I have decided the structure of my other website beforehand, but I can still add additional topic buttons if I need to. On the other hand this website has only got a few topic buttons so far, and can still go in many directions. I may do similar content, but like a newspaper or magazine, I will focus on different things... part of making the flavor of the website different and #exciting.

While I mention Suno, I also like to share stuff from YouTube and NicoNico (the Japanese Youtube). I am going to have another discussion, probably with Chatgpt, about what they mentioned about providing thumb-nails (small pictures) for the music I share, because images make all the music much more interesting. This website is a very simple design, so I can't do things that Wordpress, PearlTrees or the social networks can do. There are some technical details I need to sort out if I am going to use pictures, so this feature is still on the drawing board.

Also there are still old style music bands, solo instrumentals, and solo singers out there, but they seem to only produce stuff very occasionally, but there is no reason why they can't get a mention also.

I have yet to choose which website this one will be... 1) Old Music Fan Site Focus 2) Idols and Stars Fan Website... maybe another discussion with Chatgpt, or Grok might help develop these ideas further if needed. Or maybe 3) I will discover something unusual to do that I have not thought of yet, in my initial experiments and starting steps.

It is still early days, so I can still change my mind and do something entirely different. Since 2026-2027 is the year that our world will change, there is probably a whole lot of things to talk about on my web sites.

👀 Keep watching this space, as the mystery of what happens unfolds, who knows what will happen next!
